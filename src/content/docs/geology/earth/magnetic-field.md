# Dynamo Theory

A dynamo converts kinetic energy into electromagnetic energy.

Dynamo Theory describes how a celestial body generates its magnetic field.

In the case of Earth, convection currents of liquid metal in the outer core, driven by heat escaping from the inner core and rotated by the Coriolis effect (forming coils), generate circulating electric currents that maintain the magnetic field.

In the case of the Sun, the conductive fluid is ionized gas at the tachocline (the transition region between the radiative interior and the differentially rotating outer convective zone).

Three requisites for a dynamo:
- An electrically conductive fluid medium
- Kinetic energy provided by planetary rotation
- An internal energy source to drive convective motions within the fluid
