# Io

- tidal heating
  - energy for its volcanic activity comes from Jupiter's gravity stretching and squeezing it
  - these gravitational forces would circularize Io's orbit instead of heating it except that Io is in a three-body Laplace orbital resonance with Ganymede and Europa
    - the orbital resonance maintains Io's orbital eccentricity, causing the distance between Jupiter and Io to vary and in turn causing Io's degree of gravitational distortion to change (thus, the stretching and squeezing)
- induced magnetic field
  - Io orbits within Jupiter's magnetosphere and the molten magma ocean beneath its surface is highly conductive
    - Jupiter's magnetosphere induces a current in Io's magma ocean which creates a magnetic field
  - the poles switch twice in a Jovian day (~10 Earth hours)
- Jupiter's magnetic field generates an electric current known as the Io flux tube that couples Io's atmosphere and neutral cloud to jupiter's polar upper atmosphere
  - this creates an auroral glow in Jupiter's polar regions and aurorae in Io's atmosphere

## Core
- either fully iron or iron-sulfide
- no intrinsic magnetic field has been detected, so core is not convecting
