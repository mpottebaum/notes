# Concepts

- volcanism
  - flood volcanism / flood basalt / plateau basalt
    - result of a giant volcanic eruption of basalt lava that covers a large stretch of land or ocean floor
    - basalt -> formed from the rapid cooling of low viscosity lava rich in iron or magnesium (mafic lava)
      - more than 90% of all volcanic rock on Earth is basalt
  - lava dome
    - circular mound formed by the slow extrusion of viscous lava from a volcano
- volatile elements
  - elements that can easily transform from solid to liquid or gas
- Aeolian processes / eoulian
  - wind activity and how it shapes a planet's surface
- orbital resonance
  - when orbiting bodies exert regular, periodic gravitational influence on each other
