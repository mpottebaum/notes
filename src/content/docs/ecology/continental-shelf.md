---
title: Continental Shelf
---

- covered by terrigenous sediments (derived from rocks on land) from the erosion of continents
  - most is not from current rivers, though; 60-70% is from the last ice age when sea level was lower
- shelf seas are the areas of ocean water on the continental shelves
  - highly productive biologically due to high mixing caused by the shallower waters and enhanced current speeds
- motion is controlled by tides, wind-forcing, and brackish water (from river inflows)
